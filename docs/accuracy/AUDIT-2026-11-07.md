**MA2. A7-G2-009/010 fail because of how the tolerance is applied, not because of the engine.**

The 2′ G2 tolerance for annual positions is applied to atoms that contain the annual lagna (the Asc itself, and
every saham, since each saham adds the Asc). Those atoms are computed at the engine's own return instant. The return
instant is allowed 60 s, and the annual ascendant moves 0.24′/s (Ex 122) to 0.28′/s (Ex 118). So 60 s of allowed
return difference is 14-17′ of lagna, against a 2′ lagna tolerance. The two tolerances contradict each other.

Here the engine's return is −8 s for Ex 118 and +7 s for Ex 122, both well within 60 s. The auditor computed the
lagna with `swe` directly at PVR's printed instants:
- 1993-06-01 13:30:04 IST gives 157.2322°, +0.93′ from the printed 7 Vi 13;
- 2000-03-08 04:41:21 IST gives 280.8266°, +0.59′ from the printed 10 Cp 49.

So the lagna rule is right, and the whole difference comes from the return instant. Shifting the sahams by the same
lagna offset puts Samartha at about −1.0′ and Vanik at about −0.6′, both of which would pass.

The builder's claim stands. The fix is not to widen the tolerance: score lagna-dependent atoms at the book's printed
instant, as an input to the case. This changes rubric §6.1, so it needs a new owner sign-off (rubric §1).
