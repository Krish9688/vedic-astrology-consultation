Main windows

Three windows carry the timing in this report. Each is a period of months, not a day.

The sorting-out stretch, October 2026 to January 2027. Jupiter moves into Leo on 31 October 2026 and Rahu into Capricorn on 5 December 2026. Offers and conversations can start, but the picture is not yet settled.

The clearer opening, June 2027 to October 2027. Saturn enters Aries on 3 June 2027 and meets the natal Venus three days later, and Jupiter returns to Leo on 26 June 2027. This is the best-supported time for a move.

The handover, November 2027 to March 2028. The Rahu sub-period ends and Saturn's long period begins on 10 January 2028, with Saturn back in Aries on 23 February 2028. This is a change of tone, not only of job.

Your working pattern

Authority built through service and steady effort
Interpretation · Commentary · Parashari
What it means
You do best where responsibility grows with competence and the work has a clear purpose: roles that help people or systems function. Effort here tends to be quiet and sustained.
Likely form
Most likely a salaried or institutional path in a skilled or advisory field, with growth by taking on more responsibility rather than by a sudden leap.
Nuance
Both Saturn and Jupiter are strong: Saturn is in its own sign, and Jupiter is in the sign where it is strongest. The work drive is real.
Against it
Saturn sits in the twelfth house with Rahu, so much of the effort goes on behind the scenes. Recognition and pay can arrive later than the work deserves.
What to watch
Being given responsibility before the title or the pay changes. That is this pattern at work, not a sign of being overlooked.
That it happens: ModerateIn what form: Moderate
A work base tied to a place, a home or a structure
Interpretation · Commentary · Parashari
What it means
The ruler of the work house, Mars, sits in the house of home and roots. Work that gives you a stable base matters more than a title, and being settled supports your best work.
Likely form
A role that is anchored in one location or institution, or a move that keeps your home life steady, is more likely to suit you than constant travel or a loosely defined job.
Nuance
Mars is the strongest planet in the chart by the engine's measure, so the drive and technical or practical skill are not in doubt.
Against it
The same placement can bring friction at home or with a senior when the base feels threatened. Hasty decisions are the risk.
What to watch
How a possible move affects your home arrangements. If it unsettles them, the role is a poorer fit than it looks.
That it happens: ModerateIn what form: Weak

The chapter you are in

You are in the long Jupiter chapter, which runs from January 2012 to January 2028. It is a period of learning, service and slow growth, and it ends in about fourteen months. Within it, the Rahu sub-period has run since August 2025 and ends on 10 January 2028. Rahu tends to make things restless and to open unfamiliar doors, which fits a person asking whether to change jobs.

The Mercury inner period, which runs to 10 January 2027, adds a note of paperwork, contracts and negotiation. Mercury is weak in this chart, so the details need checking twice.

Saturn is also in the last phase of what is traditionally called the seven-and-a-half-year period of Saturn, which ends when Saturn moves into Aries on 3 June 2027. Read it as a stretch that asks for patience, not as a bad sign in itself.

Money

Income follows skill and service, but pay negotiation lags
Interpretation · Commentary · Parashari
What it means
Income comes from steady skilled or service work and from the people or institutions you serve. Windfalls are not the story.
Likely form
Gradual raises, better-paid roles over time, or income that grows as responsibility grows.
Against it
Mercury, which sits in the house of earnings, is weak here. Negotiating pay, reading contract terms and asking for what the work is worth is the weaker link.
What to watch
Whether a new role brings a clear pay change. If the terms are vague, negotiate them rather than hoping they improve.

Decisions and choices

Should you change jobs this year?
Interpretation · Commentary · Parashari
What it means
The chart does not point to this year, which has only two months left. It does point to preparing now and to a better-supported stretch next year.
Likely form
Explore, update your work and contacts, and clarify what you want from a next role. If a good offer arrives before January, check its terms carefully before deciding.
Timing
The sorting-out stretch from October 2026 to January 2027 favours looking around; the clearer opening in 2027 favours moving.
Nuance
Staying is not a failure. The Jupiter chapter is one of service and steady growth, and a considered move later is more in keeping with it than a quick exit.
Against it
Rahu in the twelfth house can make the present job feel more restrictive than it is. Restlessness alone is not a reason to move.
What to watch
Whether the wish to leave is about the role or about the time of year. If it is the role, the 2027 window is the place to act.

What is uncertain

Birth time. The birth time is taken as exact to the minute. Within the usual margin of 15 minutes the ascendant sign does not change: it would take about 67 minutes earlier or 31 later. Dasha dates also hold. The sign of the career divisional chart's ascendant would change within 8 minutes earlier or 2 later, so that chart is not used in this report.

Calculation differences.

  • Programs that count 360-day dasha years place the January 2028 change of period earlier; the sidereal-year dates are used here.

Not assessed.

  • The career divisional chart (its ascendant is not stable within the birth-time margin).
  • Employers, salary figures and offer counts: a chart does not show these.
  • Health, family and relationships are outside the scope of this report.

In practice

  • Treat the coming three months as a time to look around and prepare, not to leave.
  • If you want a move, aim for the months between June and October 2027, and get role and pay stated clearly.
  • Choose your early-2028 role deliberately: it will set the tone for Saturn's long chapter.
