# Jaimini lens (separate system; optional)

Use only when the user asks for Jaimini, or when a Parashari conclusion is Moderate and an independent, *labelled*
second opinion is useful. Jaimini is its own system with its own aspects and karakas: using its karakas inside
Parashari aspects is "like diesel fuel in a petrol engine" [LOL p122 (pr 94)]. Build a separate Jaimini evidence
note; compare it with the Parashari note only at convergence (synthesis-method §4). It is not a third independent
confirmation when it reuses the same planets and houses the Parashari note already counted.

Source standing: since 2026-09-25 the library holds BPHS (Santhanam; ch. 29–33 padas, upapada, argala, karakas,
karakamsa), Sanjay Rath's course on the Upadesa Sutras (JSR) and his *Crux* (CRUX), besides the brief material in LOL
(p85, p122, p374, p440) and HJH2 p285. These new books are searchable with `search.py --src BPHS,JSR,CRUX` but have no
source maps yet; items still marked **[EXT]** have not yet been page-checked there — check before citing.

## 1. Chara (variable) karakas

- **Ranking**: planets ranked by degrees travelled within their own sign (sign ignored): the highest is the
  Atmakaraka (AK, the self), then Amatya (AmK, mind/career counsel), Bhratri (siblings), Matri (mother), Putra
  (children), Jnati (relatives/rivals) and Dara (DK, spouse) [LOL p122; HJH2 p285].
- **7 vs 8 karakas**: LOL's scheme uses the seven planets Sun–Saturn, with Rahu only as a tie-breaker [LOL p122].
  BPHS records both schools: seven karakas (mother and child significators treated as one) and eight with Rahu added,
Rahu's degrees counted from the end of its sign [BPHS ch. 32, PDF p329–331; translator's note p338]. This is a
  **recorded dispute**: state which scheme is used; if the DK or AK changes between schemes, the karaka reading is
  Unknown, not a choice.
- **Sensitivity**: two planets within ~1° of each other in their signs can swap roles under a small longitude error;
  treat the affected karakas as unstable.
- The AK's placement is read with its **karakamsa** (the AK's navamsa sign, used as a lagna): e.g. Sun as AK aspected
  by Rahu → work connected with foreign concerns [HJH2 p285]. Karakamsa results by sign and occupant: BPHS ch. 33
[PDF p338 ff.].

## 2. Arudha padas (the image of a house)

- **Arudha Lagna (AL)**: count from the lagna to its lord; count the same number again from the lord [LOL p440, the
  Jaimini method; LOL says Parashara's more complex method gives the same results]. Examine AL "as if it were another
  birth chart" for how the person is seen and what they visibly accumulate [LOL p440].
- **Exceptions**: if the count lands in the house itself, take the 10th from it; if it lands in the 7th from the house,
  take the 4th from the original house (= the 10th from the landing point) [BPHS ch. 29 sl. 45, PDF p308]. Some
  practitioners do not apply this exception — name the convention.
- **Upapada (UL)** = arudha of the 12th house; read for marriage/partnership as an *image and continuity* layer; the
  2nd from UL for continuity [BPHS ch. 30, PDF p316–317 — the translator notes more than two views on the underlying
  sloka, so name the one used]. **A7** (arudha of the 7th) for partners and business associates, **A10** for visible career status
  [EXT, modern practice].
- Arudha is image, not substance: a strong AL with a weak lagna shows reputation ahead of resources, and vice versa
  [LOL p122 spirit; modern practice]. Never count AL as a Parashari house.

## 3. Rasi drishti and argala

- **Sign aspects**: movable signs aspect fixed signs except the adjacent one; fixed aspect movable except the
  adjacent one; dual signs aspect the other duals. Planets aspect through the signs they occupy [LOL p85 names the
  principle; details EXT Jaimini Sutras 1.1].
- **Argala (intervention)**: planets in the 2nd, 4th and 11th from a house or planet intervene; the 12th, 10th and
  3rd obstruct them respectively (virodhargala). Malefics in the 3rd also give argala [EXT Jaimini Sutras 1.1].
- Never pool rasi drishti with graha drishti in one count.

## 4. Chara dasha (sign dasha)

Jaimini timing runs through signs, not planets, and several calculation variants exist (K. N. Rao, Sanjay Rath and
others differ on direction and length) [EXT]. **Use chara dasha only from a calculation engine that names its
variant.** Never compute it by hand, and never mix its periods with Vimshottari as if they were one clock: report
them as a separate timing opinion.

## 5. How to write the Jaimini layer

"A separate Jaimini reading (a different system, used as a second opinion): the AK is … in karakamsa …; AL …; UL …
This agrees / disagrees with the Parashari picture on …; it does not add independent weight where it reuses the same
7th-house facts." Grade it with the usual five grades, inside its own note.
