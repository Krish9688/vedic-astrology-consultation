# Lal Kitab — not part of this edition

This edition of the skill does not provide Lal Kitab analysis. As a precaution about the redistribution status of Lal
Kitab source material, no Lal Kitab rules, tables, annual charts or remedies are included here.

When someone asks for a Lal Kitab reading, a Lal Kitab remedy or a yearly Lal Kitab chart:
1. Say plainly that this edition does not provide Lal Kitab analysis.
2. Do not fill the gap from memory or general knowledge, from Lal Kitab books or pages in the user's own library
   (`kg.py`, `search.py`), or from pasted text: no Lal Kitab house readings, planet states, ages, page numbers,
   remedies or annual charts. The same applies under other names ("Red Book", *totke*, Lal Kitab *upay*).
3. Offer the Parashari reading of the same question if it would help.
4. If they ask about a remedy they have heard of, apply the general safety rules in
   [remedy-safety.md](remedy-safety.md).
