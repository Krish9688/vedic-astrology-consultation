# Worked consultations (synthetic)

Teaching examples only: partial, invented chart packets. They show *reasoning and voice*, not facts about any person.
Unlisted factors are unknown. The first three are v3 examples; the v2.2 calibration examples follow unchanged.

## E1. Marriage: delay, not denial (Parashari)

**Packet.** Libra lagna. 7th Aries empty, aspected by Saturn from the 5th (Aquarius, own sign; its 3rd aspect) and by
Jupiter from the 3rd (Sagittarius, own sign; its 5th aspect). 7th lord Mars in the 10th (Cancer, debilitated), unaspected
by Jupiter (Jupiter in Sagittarius aspects Aries, Gemini and Leo). Venus (lagna lord and karaka) in the 11th, Leo.
Supplied dashas: Saturn MD; Saturn–Venus AD next year. (Checked with `chart_facts.py`.)

**Answer (voice).**
"Marriage is clearly promised here, but it has been set up to come later rather than early — this looks like delay, not
denial. The reason I say that: Jupiter aspects the seventh house from its own sign, and Venus, which is both your
lagna lord and the natural significator of partnership, is well placed in the house of fulfilment. Against that, Saturn
also looks at the seventh, and the seventh lord Mars is debilitated in the tenth, so commitment tends to arrive through
work or responsibility and after some hesitation.

Saturn is your yogakaraka, so its own aspect on the seventh is not the hostile kind. The timing becomes more interesting
in the Saturn–Venus period next year: Saturn already touches the seventh, and Venus is the karaka and your lagna lord.
Both periods point at the same house — Raman calls this the strongest kind of activation. I'd treat the promise as Strong
and the timing as Moderate until we check the transits for that window."

*Why this is good:* answers first; three decisive links; the counter-weight explained, not listed; lordship over natural
nature (Saturn as yogakaraka); timing from significator intensity; grades named in plain words.

## Earlier calibration examples (v2.2, retained)

These v2.2 examples predate the evidence-grade vocabulary; read "High/Moderate/Low" as Strong/Moderate/Weak.


Confidence labels describe internal astrological support, not empirically established odds. Nominal varga mappings are checked, but birth-time stability is not established by these partial packets. Varga interpretations below are therefore conditional; they do not raise confidence as though they were verified stable confirmations.

## 1. Career potential activated: support without a guaranteed promotion

**Question:** “Does the supplied Saturn–Venus period support advancement?”

**Fact packet:** Taurus D1 ascendant 15°; Saturn Aquarius 1°; Venus Aquarius 4°. Saturn rules 9/10 and Venus 1/6; they are associated in the 10th. Standard D9: ascendant Taurus, Saturn Libra, Venus Scorpio. Standard D10: ascendant Gemini, Saturn Aquarius (9th), Venus Pisces (10th). The packet identifies a Saturn–Venus AD and a hypothetical Jupiter contact to the D1 10th during months 6–8 of that AD. No actual ephemeris, exact AD calendar boundaries, Sun separation or full chart was supplied.

**Prediction:** This combination gives meaningful support for professional advancement during the supplied period, but a promotion is one possible expression rather than an assured outcome.

**Why:** Saturn joins career and training/mentorship through its 9th/10th ownership and is in its own 10th-house sign. Venus connects the person and service/work obligations to that same place. Their association brings these themes together; the yogakaraka label does not add a separate confirmation. Saturn's D9 exaltation supports its capacity, while D10 places it in its own sign and Venus exalted in the professional 10th. Those derived charts provide confirmation with shared-input dependence, not separate experiments.

**Moderation:** Venus also carries 6th-house duties and competition. Its D9 position is not an additional exaltation. Unknown solar proximity and other planets prevent an unqualified strong-chart verdict. Greater responsibility can precede greater reward.

**Activation and trigger:** MD Saturn foregrounds the career network; AD Venus directly participates through conjunction and ownership. The hypothetical Jupiter contact makes months 6–8 a candidate refinement **only if a real ephemeris and period table verify it**.

**Manifestation:** Expanded duties, a better role or recognition linked to service/training. Alternatives include an internal reassignment or a move requiring more work without immediate pay improvement.

**Confidence:** Event (advancement potential in the period): Moderate because the selected network is supportive but the full chart is absent. Timing: Moderate for the stipulated broad period; calendar trigger timing Not assessable. Manifestation as a promotion: Moderate. Verified full-chart support and actual dates could change these judgments.

## 2. D1–D10 conflict limits the promotion claim

**Question:** “Does a strong 10th-house Saturn guarantee promotion?”

**Packet:** Taurus ascendant 15°; Saturn Aquarius 8°. Standard D9 Saturn Sagittarius; standard D10 ascendant Gemini, Saturn Aries (11th), its debilitation sign. Saturn period is stipulated but AD and transits are missing.

**Prediction:** The D1 supports a strong professional focus, but an uncomplicated promotion is less clear.

Saturn's own Aquarius in the 10th and 9th/10th ownership support career capacity. In D10, it reaches the gains house in debilitation, so gains or recognition may come with obstacles or reduced control. Neither the 11th position nor the D1 yoga label cancels that condition. There is no supplied neechabhanga evidence.

A demanding assignment, delayed recognition or increased work before reward is a plausible alternative. Event confidence for professional emphasis: Moderate. Manifestation as promotion: Low given the material varga conflict and incomplete data. Timing: Not assessable without AD boundaries and triggers. A verified stable D10 and actual cancellation/support would materially affect the interpretation.

## Revision 2: synthetic failure and consultation examples

These additional packets are teaching data, not physical ephemerides, personal charts or evidence of prediction accuracy. Retain the prior worked examples above as complementary cases.

### Incorrect premise with downstream conclusions

Input: “Virgo lagna, Jupiter in Virgo, debilitated; therefore formal study is denied and a Jupiter remedy is required.”

Response: Jupiter's listed sign is not its conventional debilitation sign. The debilitation, denial and remedy chain is unusable. For the stated lagna Jupiter rules 4/7 and occupies 1; those unaffected relationships may be discussed with the rest of the supplied chart. Do not simply change “debilitated” to “strong,” and do not fabricate missing education evidence.

### Cross-boundary timing

Input: a hypothetical authoritative source assigns subperiod A to an interval ending 2034-04-01 and subperiod B from that date; narrative says A governs a forecast through 2035.

Response: retain the original table's end-exclusive boundary, flag the narrative inconsistency and analyze each subperiod separately if its natal links are supplied. A broad transition hypothesis may span both, but it cannot borrow A's rationale after A ends. The fictional dates demonstrate consistency, not dasha arithmetic.

### Corrected historical context

Input: an already-known move was explained under period X. The user corrects the move date into period Y.

Response: mark the X-based historical fit superseded and dependent forward claims stale. Reassess Y using the original chart facts; do not call either hindsight explanation a forecast hit. Unaffected natal themes remain available. A revised explanation may remain plausible without gaining confidence.

### Outcome review

Original claim: “An employment offer will arrive by 2032-06-30.” Later report: no offer by that deadline. Record a miss, preserve the original deadline and review the evidence before a new forecast. By contrast, “This period may feel active” lacks a defined event and is not evaluable as an offer forecast. Do not score either as a success because learning occurred.

### Consultation and cross-domain tension

Partial hypothetical packet: Libra lagna, Moon in Cancer, Saturn in Capricorn; only these D1 facts are supplied. The person asks about taking more responsibility while studying. Moon owns/occupies 10; Saturn owns 4/5 and occupies 4, with its 7th aspect to Cancer. No dates, reliable Vargas or strength totals supplied.

“The supplied facts connect work responsibility with sustained learning. Moon's role in the tenth house makes career a relevant theme, while Saturn connects foundational learning and judgment with that same work sector through its aspect. A larger assignment is one possibility, but these few placements cannot establish a promotion or its timing. Since you are also studying, workload and study time deserve consideration together. That practical tradeoff comes from your stated situation; it is not proof that one part of life must suffer.”

Theme confidence: Moderate for this limited network; timing: Not assessable; promotion as the exact expression: Low. No stable varga confirmation is available. Additional relevant D1 facts and dated MD/AD data could materially change the reading.

### Evidence-preserving rewrite

Working claim: “Professional responsibilities may change during the supplied broad period. Employer change remains one of two alternatives. Event Moderate; timing Low; no verified transit.”

Natural version: “This period may bring a change in responsibilities. That could happen with your current employer or through a move, and the supplied evidence does not clearly favor one. The broad theme has moderate support within the reading; timing remains weak because transit refinement is unavailable.” Do not add an exact month or promote this to a strong job-offer forecast during editing.

Mark the old event-to-period fit stale, retain the correction in the outcome record and re-evaluate its new period relation. Also revisit any claim that the earlier contract now prepares a career culmination, including summaries.
