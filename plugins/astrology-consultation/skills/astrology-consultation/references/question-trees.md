# Question trees: which techniques a question actually needs

Each tree is the **order of judgement** for one family of questions, with a stop rule at each step. Walk only the
branches the question needs; the rules themselves live in the topic files, [timing.md](timing.md) and
[vargas.md](vargas.md). The reasoning discipline (roles, promise classes, scenarios, confidence) is
[consultation-reasoning.md](consultation-reasoning.md). "P / S / X" marks a step as **Primary** (can set the
direction), **Secondary** (sets degree or quality) or **Supporting** (texture or fine timing only; can never
overturn a primary verdict) [R1-P3].

## 1. Relationship (romance, dating, a serious relationship)

First decide the **event class** — the tree differs:

| Event class | Houses / factors that carry it |
|---|---|
| Attraction, dating, casual connection | 5th, its lord, Venus; the lagna lord's link to the 5th |
| Emotional bond, "the person" | 5th + 7th joined; Moon (feeling), Venus |
| Serious / committed relationship | 7th, its lord, Venus; the 2nd/11th joining it = becomes family/lasting [R1-P9] |
| Engagement / marriage | → tree 2 |
| Friendship becoming romance | 11th ↔ 5th/7th links; Mercury |
| Reconnection with a past person | 12th/8th links, Ketu, retrograde significators, a period lord active in the earlier episode |
| Short or unstable bond | 5th/7th activation without 2nd/11th; Rahu/Ketu or dusthana lord as the activator |

Order:
1. **P — Promise for that class**: the triad of the class's house(s), from lagna and Moon. Class per
   consultation-reasoning §3.
2. **S — Quality**: Venus's condition (comfort, attraction pattern), Moon (emotional availability), D9 Venus and D9
   7th for durability. A strained Venus with an intact triad is *quality*, not *delay*.
3. **P — Activation**: are the running MD/AD lords connected to the class's houses or to Venus? Grade with Raman's
   tiers (both / one / neither).
4. **X/S — Trigger**: Jupiter/Saturn contacts to the same network inside the active AD; PD only to narrow a window
   that steps 3–4 already support.
5. **S — Form**: the route and type of connection (§3 below).
6. **Scenarios**: new person / existing connection deepens / past person / attraction without commitment — rank them.
7. **Synthesis**: class, likeliest form, broad activation → sharper window, confidence per claim.
Stop rule: if step 1 gives *weakly supported*, answer mostly from steps 1–3 and do not manufacture a window.

## 2. Marriage (promise, timing, relationship vs marriage)

1. **P — Promise**: 7th + 7th lord + Venus (Jupiter for a woman's chart in classical texts; use the karaka the chart
   and person make relevant), from lagna and Moon; **P — D9**: D9 7th house, its lord, D9 lagna lord and Venus's D9
   condition decide durability and can veto only if all afflicted with no rescuer [R1-P7]. 2nd (family addition) and
   11th (fulfilment) joining the 7th make it *marriage* rather than *relationship*.
2. **Promise class** incl. *modified* (distance, different background, unconventional form).
3. **P — Activation**: periods of the 7th lord, Venus, planets in/aspecting the 7th, the dispositors of the 7th lord,
   the 2nd/11th lords when linked — *at a usable age*; mark which are par-excellence (both lords linked) [HJH2 p25–26;
   PD X.13].
4. **S — Trigger**: Jupiter to the 7th lord's sign/navamsa or their trines [PM2 XX.66]; Raman's lagna-lord + 7th-lord
   point [HJH2 p26]; Saturn's influence as the "approver" is a common school claim but near-always true — supporting only
   [R1-P8].
5. **Relationship timing vs marriage timing**: relationship activity follows 5th/7th activation and Venus periods; a
   *marriage* window needs the 7th plus 2nd/11th (or D9) in the same period. Say both when they differ.
6. **Synthesis**: promise class; first strong window at a usable age; later windows if the first passes; what form.

## 3. How will I meet them / who initiates (description of form)

These are **form** questions: rank candidate routes, don't pick the first house that fits.
1. List candidate routes from the 7th lord's (and 5th lord's) house and associations: 11th friends/networks/groups;
   3rd messaging, neighbourhood, short trips, siblings; 5th study, creative or leisure settings; 9th higher study,
   long travel, teachers, religion; 10th work; 12th abroad, online/distant, private; 6th colleagues/service; 4th home,
   family circle; 1st direct personal encounter.
2. For each, gather support that is **independent of the first placement**: the dispositor's house, the D9 7th lord's
   house, the house the running period lords activate, the transit contacts in the window, the Moon chart. A route
   that appears in two or more channels wins; a route from one placement is "possible".
3. **Who initiates** has no credible method in the library or in practice [R3-RP6]. Say so in one sentence, then
   answer the nearest useful question: does the current period favour the person taking initiative (strong lagna
   lord; Mars/Venus or 3rd/5th/7th-linked periods; the lagna lord activated by transit)?
4. Answer with the winning route, the runner-up, and honest confidence (usually Weak–Moderate).

## 4. Future spouse: nature and appearance

1. **P — Nature**: the stronger of Venus, the 7th lord and any 7th-house occupant [PM2 XX.21]; the planet owning the
   7th sign or its navamsa (temperament, age, fortune) [BJ p243]; D9 7th and its lord; planets joined to Venus
   [PM2 XX.17–18]. Keep a trait only when two indicators agree.
2. **S — Appearance**: the stronger of the 7th lord's dispositor and its navamsa lord [PM2 XX.20], described through
   the planet body-types [BJ p52–53; PD p42–44] as **themes and ranges** — never measurements, complexion rankings,
   ethnicity or caste; say plainly this is the least reliable output [R3-AP3, AP4].
3. **X — Jaimini** (separate note): Darakaraka and its sign quality.
4. Synthesis: two or three repeated traits, then one qualifier. Confidence: Moderate at best for nature, Weak for looks.

## 5. Career (change, direction, promotion, job vs business)

1. Split: vocation type / a change in the horizon / promotion or status / job vs business / income (→ tree 6).
2. **P — Promise**: lagna and lagna lord (the capacity to act), the 10th, its lord, karakas (Sun status, Saturn work,
   Mercury trade/skill, Jupiter advice/teaching), from lagna, Moon and Sun [HJH2 p253; topic-career-money §3].
3. **S — D10**: confirms or qualifies within the career domain; only with a stable D10 lagna [vargas.md].
4. **S — Supporting houses**: 6th (service, competition, employment), 7th (business, clients), 2nd/11th (earnings,
   gains), 3rd (initiative, media), 12th (foreign/institutional).
5. **P — Activation**: MD/AD lords linked to the 10th/its lord/D10; a period lord in a dusthana or linked to the 12th
   can mean change of place rather than loss.
6. **X — Trigger**: Saturn and Jupiter on the 10th, its lord, the lagna lord or the Moon.
7. Scenarios: role change within the organisation / new employer / independent work / further study before a move.

## 6. Money

Income (11th, its lord) vs savings and family wealth (2nd) vs expenses (12th) vs sudden gains/losses (8th); Jupiter
and the 2nd/11th lords as karakas; D2 only as a secondary lens; activation by 2nd/11th-linked periods. Never
investment signals [topic-career-money.md].

## 7. Travel, study abroad and relocation

1. Split: short trips (3rd) / long journeys with return (9th) / residence away (12th, with the 4th "releasing" the
   person) / study abroad (9th + 4th/5th education) / work abroad (10th + 12th) [R1-P9; HJH2 p464–465].
2. **P — Promise** for the class: 12th and its lord, 9th and its lord, the 4th's strength (a strong, undisturbed 4th
   keeps a person rooted), movable vs fixed signs, Rahu's placement and links.
3. **S — D4** for residence (stable time only); **S — D9** for long-distance marriage links.
4. **P — Activation**: periods of 12th/9th-linked planets and Rahu; a lagna lord in the 6th gives journeys, not long
   residence [HJH2 p464–465].
5. **X — Trigger**: Saturn/Jupiter/Rahu transits over the 9th/12th or their lords.
6. Scenarios: trip / temporary stint / study / settlement; say which is favoured and what would change it (visas,
   jobs and choices are real-world factors).

## 8. Open activation: "what is active now", "next 30 days", "the theme of my period", "what am I overlooking"

Do **not** tour the planets. Build an activation list, rank it, answer from the top [R3-DP1–DP6; R2-TP15].
1. **Chapter — MD lord, read whole**: what it owns (the subject), where it sits (how it lands), who joins or aspects it
   (who else is involved); read from the lagna (events) and the Moon (how it feels).
2. **Episode — AD lord**: the same, plus its house counted from the MD lord. When MD and AD lords point to the same kind
   of house, expect a clear theme; when they point different ways, expect continuation rather than a new start.
3. **Snapshot across vargas**: where the MD and AD lords sit in D9 (relationships) and D10 (work) — kendras/trines =
   steady progress; 3/6/8 = strain, turned to growth by good dignity or to loss by poor dignity. Stable times only.
4. **Weeks** (30-day questions): the PD lord inside the AD, plus slow transits (Saturn, Jupiter, nodes) on the lagna,
   Moon or period lords this month. No slow contact → a *texture* month; say no event-level timing is indicated.
   Without transit data for the month, say that layer is unknown.
5. **Rank domains** (relationships, career, money, study, travel, friends, family, home, conflict, routines,
   spirituality) by how many *distinct* entries hit them. Top one or two = the theme; transit-only = background.
   Don't show the scores.
6. **"Unexpected"**: the top domain surfacing through Rahu/Ketu, the 8th, a station or an eclipse contact *if
   present*; otherwise say the month looks like development in the top domains rather than a shock.
7. **"What am I overlooking?"**: a strongly activated domain the person did not ask about (top two only; never health
   scares) — one line, as a suggestion [R3-DP4].
8. Every theme gets a domain, a direction (build / strain / release / change), a window and **one observable early
   sign** so it can be checked [R3-DP6].

## 9. Explaining a past period ("why was X hard?")

1. Identify the MD/AD (and PD if short) of that window; read each lord whole and from each other.
2. Check the slow transits of that window if data are supplied; otherwise say so.
3. Name the domains the period activated and how their condition would show as strain; separate "difficult for you"
   from "difficult events".
4. Frame as *consistent with the chart*, not proof; ask what actually happened if the answer depends on it — and use
   the answer as calibration (consultation-reasoning §8), not to retune the method.

## 10. Children, property, education, health symbolism

Use the topic files' networks with the same skeleton — promise (house/lord/karaka, Moon), relevant varga with a stable
time (D7, D4, D24; health only as symbolism), activation, trigger, scenarios, confidence — and their safety limits
(no fertility, pregnancy, diagnosis or legal verdicts).
