# Consultation reasoning: from the client's question to a judgement

This is the thinking layer. The other references hold rules; this file decides **which rules matter for this
question, what role each factor plays, and how they combine into one judgement**. The research behind it (how
experienced practitioners actually reason, with sources and critiques) is in
`_skill_workspace/docs/ASTROLOGER-CONSULTATION-RESEARCH.md`; tags like `[R1-P3]` point there. Book tags as usual.

## 0. Two layers: the workbench and the answer

Do the reasoning in a private **workbench** (your own thinking, never pasted into the reply). The client sees only
the judgement and the few reasons that carry it (§10). A good consultation may examine thirty facts and show four.
The workbench has a fixed shape:

```
Q  (as asked) → Q* (answerable form) → event class, horizon, what would count as "yes"
PROMISE   class + the 2–3 facts that decide it          (§3)
ACTIVATION the periods inside the horizon, graded       (§4)
TRIGGER   slow-transit contacts inside those periods    (§4)
ROLES     each remaining factor → its role              (§2)
SCENARIOS 2–4 manifestations, each with its support     (§5)
CONFLICTS what controls what                            (§6)
CONFIDENCE per claim: event / timing / manifestation    (§7)
EXPOSE    what the client needs to see, in what order   (§10)
```

## 1. Understand the question before touching the chart

1. **Restate it in answerable form** [R1-P9]. "Will I find love?" → "Is a committed relationship indicated in the
   next two years, and in what form?" "Will I go abroad?" → "Travel, study, a work stint, or settling?"
2. **Classify it** — the class decides the technique budget:

| Class | Examples | Core of the answer |
|---|---|---|
| Promise | "Is marriage promised?", "Will I ever own a house?" | Natal promise class (§3), then whether a usable-age period exists |
| Timing | "When will I marry?", "When does my career move?" | Promise → activation → trigger ladder (§4) |
| Open activation | "What is active now?", "Next 30 days?", "Theme of my period?", "What am I overlooking?" | Activation scan across all houses ([question-trees.md](question-trees.md) §8) |
| Description | "What will my spouse be like / look like?", "How will I meet?" | Scenario ranking over form/quality factors (§5) |
| Comparison / choice | "New person or someone from the past?", "Who initiates?" | Scenario ranking; honest "the chart barely separates these" when true |
| Explanation of the past | "Why was 2025 hard?" | Period analysis of that past window; *consistent with*, never proof [R1-P10] |

3. **Fit it to the person**: age, stage of life and culture decide what an activation can mean — a 7th-house period
   at 21 is more often a serious relationship than a wedding; "late" is relative [HJH2 p32; HPA p75; LOL p358].
4. **Intent matters where outcomes depend on choice** (marriage vs contentment, settling vs travelling): ask one
   short question if the answer would change, otherwise state the assumption [R1-P9].
5. **Red-flag scan**: even for a narrow question, glance at the whole current period for anything serious the person
   should hear about (with the usual safety limits) [R1-P9, K. N. Rao's own failure case].

Then pick the tree in [question-trees.md](question-trees.md). The coverage checklist in
[synthesis-method.md](synthesis-method.md) §1a is a **safety net for full readings only**, not a procedure to recite.

## 2. Give every factor a role before a meaning

The mechanical error is "factor → house meaning → prediction". Instead, first decide what a factor is *doing* in
this question. A factor can play one of these roles:

| Role | Question it answers | Typical carriers |
|---|---|---|
| **Promise** | Can it happen at all, and how firmly? | House + lord + karaka as one judgement, from lagna and Moon; the relevant varga |
| **Capacity** | How much can the promising planet deliver? | Dignity, dispositor, strength (the right measure for the purpose) |
| **Quality / experience** | What is it like to live? | Natural benefic/malefic influence, karaka condition, D9 for relationships |
| **Form / route** | In what shape, where, through whom? | Sign and house of the lord, its associations, Rahu/Ketu, dusthana links |
| **Obstruction** | Delay, cost, or conditions attached | Saturn, dusthana lords, affliction *to the promise itself* |
| **Activation** | When is it foregrounded? | Dasha and bhukti lords connected to the matter |
| **Trigger** | Which months inside an active period? | Slow transits over the same network |
| **Context** | What is realistic for this person? | Age, circumstances, stated past events |

**Assignment rules** (the heart of senior judgement):

- **Lordship decides the event; natural nature colours the experience** [R1-P3; LOL p304]. A benefic that rules bad
  houses feels pleasant and still brings trouble; a malefic ruling good houses feels hard and still delivers.
- **A negative factor is not automatically "delay"** [R1-P5]. List its candidate readings — *delay, quality,
  dynamics, attraction pattern, setting, intention* — and keep only those another independent factor supports:
  - becomes **delay** only when it also touches the timing layer (it is or aspects the running period's lords, or
    sits on house, lord *and* karaka together);
  - becomes **quality/dynamics** when the promise is intact but the karaka or D9 is strained (seriousness, distance,
    duty, an age or background gap);
  - becomes **form/setting** when it links to the 12th, 9th or Rahu (abroad, unconventional, a different community);
  - becomes **attraction pattern** when it sits on the karaka or the lagna lord (who the person is drawn to);
  - never becomes denial on its own (§3).
- **Weak karaka, strong house** → the matter happens with a specific flaw in the karaka's domain ("the occasional
  fly"). **Strong karaka, weak house/lord** → desire and attraction are there; external delivery is slower or
  obstructed [LOL p279 (house good, lord bad → both results occur); the strong-karaka case is
  a practitioner inference from the objective/subjective split, LOL p304 — R1-P4]. A single strength such as
  exaltation does not outweigh many weaknesses [LOL p289].
- **Strength limits how far delay can go.** A strong lord and karaka "cannot push it off too far" even under
  Saturn; one strong protective factor prevents the worst without guaranteeing happiness [HJH2 p32, p46, p60].
- **Saturn is not an afflicter when it rules the matter** (7th lord, yogakaraka, lagna lord) [HJH2 p33, p40, p216].
- **Rahu/Ketu describe form more than verdict**: an unusual route, background, suddenness or distance [LOL p401].
- **Common placements carry little information** (Saturn–Venus contacts, Mars in 1/2/4/7/8/12, a node on the 1–7
  axis): note their base rate before giving them weight [R1-P5].
- **A yoga only counts in the periods of its own planets** [R1-P3; LOL p310].

## 3. Establish the natal promise (a class, not yes/no)

Judge house + lord + karaka as **one** triad, from the lagna and again from the Moon, then the relevant varga
([synthesis-method.md](synthesis-method.md) §2). Place the result in one class [R1-P2]:

| Class | When | How it shapes the answer |
|---|---|---|
| **Promised** | Triad mostly supportive from lagna and Moon, varga does not contradict | Timing questions are about *when*, not *whether* |
| **Promised with delay** | Promise intact; obstruction touches the timing layer | Give the window after the obstruction; "delay, not denial" |
| **Modified** | Promise intact; form/setting factors strong (12th, Rahu, dusthana links) | Say *what form* is likeliest |
| **Weakly supported** | One or two secondary supports only | Possible, not indicated; timing claims inherit the weakness |
| **Not supported** | ≥ 3 independent channels afflicted, including the varga arbiter (D9 7th and its lord for marriage), no strong rescuer | Rare. Say "the chart does not clearly show…", never "never" |

Map this to *Light on Life*'s karma intensity: fixed (dridha) where most factors agree — predict firmly; mixed
(dridha-adridha) — results respond to effort; unmarked (adridha) — say the chart leaves it open [LOL p57–59, p64–65].

## 4. Activation and trigger: "promised" is not "happening now"

Keep the two statements separate in every timing answer: *the chart promises X* and *X is active in this window*.
Full technique in [timing.md](timing.md); the reasoning rules are:

1. **Scale match** [R1-P1]: mahadasha = the chapter (years, themes); antardasha = the episode (the main timing unit
   for events); pratyantardasha = narrowing *inside* an already confirmed antardasha window, never on its own;
   deeper levels explain everything in hindsight and predict nothing [LOL p354].
2. **Read the dasha lord whole before its role** [R1-P6]: its dignity, the houses it owns/occupies/aspects, its
   yogas; everything it touches will be active, not just the question's house.
3. **Relevance tiers** (Raman): both MD and AD lords connected to the matter → the matter "par excellence"; one
   connected → limited or nominal; neither → the event is not foregrounded now, however good the transits
   [HJH2 via R1-P3; timing.md §2].
4. **Transits trigger, they do not create** [R1-P1]. Jupiter/Saturn contacts to the same network *inside an active
   period* narrow the window to months. A transit in an inactive period gives activity without outcome (talks,
   meetings, near-misses) [R1-P4].
5. **Discount conditions that are true most of the time** [R1-P8]: double transit over "the 7th or its lord from
   lagna or Moon", Jupiter aspecting something relevant, any OR-bundle of three or more conditions. They are
   supporting evidence, never the reason for a window.
6. **Granularity follows evidence**: a broad activation (years) is stated as such; a sharp window (a few months) needs
   period + transit agreeing on the same network; a month needs all layers plus a validated birth time. Never a day.

## 5. Scenarios: what could this activation actually be?

An activation is a class of events, not one event. Before answering, generate 2–4 concrete manifestations and test
each against the chart:

1. **List** plausible forms for this person (e.g. a 7th-house activation at 21: a new serious relationship; an
   existing connection becoming committed; a past person returning; strong attraction that does not become
   commitment; a business or study partnership).
2. **Score** each by the channels that specifically favour it — not by how many factors "fit" loosely. Examples:
   reconnection is favoured by the 12th/8th, retrograde or Ketu involvement, a period lord that was active in the
   earlier episode; a *new* person by the 5th/7th/11th and Rahu; commitment by the 2nd and 11th joining the 7th and a
   supportive D9 [R1-P9].
3. **Choose** the best-supported scenario, keep the runner-up, and say why the others are weaker. If two are close,
   say that the chart does not separate them — that is information too.

Choosing among a combination's meanings uses house, sign, the planets' natures and lordships, other influences and
the person's environment together — the same Mars–Mercury can make a debater, a liar or a boxer [LOL p308–309].

## 6. Contradictions: decide what each factor controls

Contradictions usually **add up rather than cancel** [LOL p279, p342, p396; R1-P4]. Resolve by assigning control:

| Conflict | What controls what |
|---|---|
| Strong 7th house, weak Venus | House/lord control *occurrence*; Venus controls *comfort and attraction* → it happens, with a relational flaw to name |
| Marriage-type period active, Saturn transit on the network | Period controls *whether in this window*; Saturn controls *pace and seriousness* → slower, more deliberate, not cancelled |
| Good D10, difficult D1 10th | D1 = the concrete platform; D10 = the career domain → both show, in different sub-periods; the dasha partner decides which surfaces [R1-P4] |
| Excellent Jupiter transit, unrelated period | Period controls; the transit gives opportunity-shaped activity without the event |
| Promise strong, no relevant period in horizon | Promise stands; timing is outside this horizon — say when the next relevant period starts |
| Two period lords pulling opposite ways | Judge their relative strength; confident prediction is harder — say so [R1-P4] |
| Lordship vs natural nature | Lordship → what happens; nature → how it feels [LOL p304] |

Only when no assignment resolves it, grade **Contradictory** and say what each side predicts
([synthesis-method.md](synthesis-method.md) §5 for the procedure; [contradiction-register.md](contradiction-register.md)
when the *sources* disagree).

## 7. Confidence: built from quality, not quantity

Use the five grades (Strong / Moderate / Weak / Contradictory / Unknown), set separately for **event**, **timing** and
**manifestation**. Decide them from these, in this order:

1. **Promise class** (§3) — caps everything: weakly supported promise → at most Weak for timed claims.
2. **Independent channels** — count D1 triad, Moon-chart repeat, relevant varga, dasha link, slow transit and
   (separately) Lal Kitab/Jaimini by their own mechanisms. D9 and its derivatives are one channel; MD+AD is one;
   the same transit counted from lagna and Moon is one [R1-P8].
3. **Timing convergence** — activation *and* trigger on the same network.
4. **Directness** — is the event represented directly (7th lord's period for partnership) or inferred (11th = "met
   through friends")? Inferred claims drop a grade.
5. **Birth-time stability** of every fact used (D9 lagna, D10, house cusps).
6. **Source tier** of the rules carrying the claim (classical and repeated > one modern author > synthesis).
7. **Unresolved contradictions** and **base rates** (common placements, near-always-true transits) lower it.

**Grade the promise on the whole triad.** Roles decide *how* a weakness shows (quality, pace, form); they do not
remove it from the grade. A materially weak karaka (below its strength minimum, in an enemy sign, or afflicted by a
malefic) or an empty/unsupported house caps the *promise* at Moderate even when the lord is strong — say "promised,
but not emphatic" rather than Strong.

**Every window names its layer.** A narrower window inside a broad activation must carry its basis in the same
sentence ("May–October 2027, the Venus sub-sub-period inside the Jupiter period"); if it has no stateable basis,
leave it out. A period chosen as *the* marriage or career window must have its lord linked to that network
specifically (Raman's tiers), not only to a neighbouring house.

**Keep facts at their true precision.** Describe contacts by orb: within about 1° "exact", 1–3° "close", beyond
that "in range"; never "almost exactly" for 2°+. Say whether a slow transit is *applying* (moving toward the natal
point) or *separating* — a retrograde planet moving away is not "passing over" it. When you flag birth-time
sensitivity, name the part that is sensitive (the D10 rising sign and houses) and the part that is not (the planets'
D10 signs). Don't project a transit beyond the dates you have. Superlatives
("strongest house", "the main planet") only when the complete set of values was compared, and name what was compared
("Jupiter rather than Saturn, the two planets this rule compares").

Many weak indicators never add up to Strong. Before finalising, run two checks [R1-P12]: *would I read this evidence
the same way if the person hoped for the opposite?* and *is any of this true for most charts?*

## 8. Past events as calibration, not as proof

If the person has shared dated events (or you ask one *discriminating* question — "did 2019 bring a move or a change
of study?" — when fine timing matters; never fish): fix the method first, then
check 3–5 well-separated events of different kinds; report "consistent with N of M"; use the result to decide how
much weight birth-time-sensitive factors (D9/D10 lagna, pratyantardashas) deserve. Never switch dasha variants,
ayanamsa or house systems after seeing the outcome to make it fit [R1-P10, P11].

## 9. Systems stay separate

Parashari, Jaimini, KP, Nadi (Deva Keralam, DEVA2) and Lal Kitab each reach their own verdict by their own rules; agreement between them is
reported as convergence ([synthesis-method.md](synthesis-method.md) §4.4), never computed by mixing their aspects or
significators [R1-P13; LOL p122]. In the answer, a Lal Kitab or Jaimini note earns space only when it confirms,
changes or sharpens the conclusion; if it is mixed or silent, one sentence ("Lal Kitab doesn't add anything decisive
here") or nothing.

## 10. Turning the judgement into the answer

Choose the mode ([communication.md](communication.md) §0) and use its shape — for a focused answer §1: conclusion →
likeliest form → timing → the main nuance → the few decisive reasons → an early sign to watch → confidence (technical
basis and page citations only when asked). Translate each workbench finding into its life consequence before writing
it (§1a: the subject of the sentence is the person's life, the chart fact follows). The workbench
decides what is *true enough to say*; the answer decides what the person *needs to hear*. A focused answer names
about four chart facts at most; never chain "because X is in Y… because Z aspects W".

Before sending, reread the draft as the client: does the first paragraph answer my question? Would I know what to
expect, when, and how sure the astrologer is? Is anything there only because it was on the workbench?
