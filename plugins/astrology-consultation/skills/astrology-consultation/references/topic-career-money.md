# Career, business, income and wealth

Load for questions about vocation, job change, promotion, business, income, savings, windfalls, debt or financial
stress. Procedure from [synthesis-method.md](synthesis-method.md); timing from [timing.md](timing.md). No investment,
trading or market-timing advice: astrology here describes themes, not returns.

## 1. Split the question

*Type* of work (vocation) · *rank/success* (how far) · *change* (when and what kind) · *income* (inflow) ·
*retention/wealth* (accumulation) · *losses/debt*. Raman: yogas raise the degree of success but do not show the type of
work [HJH2 p288]; more yogas mean more distinction; judge rank separately from type [HJH2 p304–321].

## 2. Capacities first

Before vocation, gauge mental, intellectual and physical capacity from the Sun, Moon/Mercury and lagna; heavy Saturn/
Rahu affliction to the Moon or Mercury suits less high-pressure responsibility [HJH2 p249]. Mercury matters as much as
the Moon for career judgement in Raman's experience [HJH2 p249].

## 3. Vocation (type of work)

- **Three reference points**: the 10th from lagna, Moon and Sun — use the strongest [PD V.1; HJH2 p253; BJ p148].
- **The 10th-lord navamsa dispositor** shows the nature of work [PD V.1–8; HJH2 p253, p267]. Raman's choice of the
  "vocational determinant" [HJH2 p253]:
  - 10th empty: stronger of the 10th lord and the lord of the navamsa it occupies;
  - one planet in the 10th: strongest of 10th lord, that occupant, and the 10th lord's navamsa dispositor;
  - several: the strongest (if two are conjunct, the stronger of their navamsa lords); roughly equal → more than one
    occupation. Exclude a 10th occupant sitting on the bhava-sandhi [HJH2 p255]. Planets aspecting an empty 10th count;
    exact-degree aspects to the 10th cusp dominate [HJH2 p303–310].
- **Honest limit**: in Raman's own study the 10th decided the profession clearly in only 15 of 50+ charts; the chart's
  strongest planet or sign often decides [HJH2 p250]. So offer a *field of aptitude*, not a job title.
- **Karakas and planet groups** [HJH2 p251–252, p260]: Jupiter/Mercury intellectual, Venus aesthetic, Sun/Moon/Mars
  economic and executive, Mercury trade, Saturn labour/industry/law/persistence, nodes routine or unusual/technical
  work (Rahu: chemicals, industries). The Sun is the working career karaka and "political planet"; one table Raman quotes
  gives Jupiter as 10th karaka (CR-V09). Sun or Mars touching intellectual planets → service under the state.
- **Sign on the 10th**: fiery engineering/energy; airy intellectual; earthy practical/administrative; watery liquids,
  biology, shipping; movable enterprise and sales, fixed research and perseverance [HJH2 p268–269].
- **Varga**: HJH2 does not use D10; it uses the navamsa. HPA notes Parashara's dasamsa for profession [HPA p28]. Use a
  supplied, time-stable D10 as confirmation of the 10th lord's condition ([vargas.md](vargas.md)); do not let D10 alone
  create a career event.
- Translate classical occupations (PD V.2–8, XVIII) into contemporary *functions* (analysis, care, logistics,
  persuasion, building, law, teaching), never a guaranteed title.

## 4. Rank, recognition and setbacks

Markers of rise: 9th–10th lord link; lagna lord–10th lord relationship ("always fortifying"); mahapurusha yoga by the
10th lord; 10th lord exalted aspecting the 10th [HJH2 p304–321]. Markers of fall or instability: Saturn in the 10th
(rise then fall) [HJH2 p362]; raja yoga in the 12th or 6th, or 12th from the Moon [HJH2 p357–366]; Saturn–Venus mutual
periods break raja yogas [HJH2 p364]. Lal Kitab's "deceitful 10th" says the same kind of thing in its own terms (below).

## 5. Income and wealth

- **Income** = 11th (gains) with its lord and occupants; source from the 11th occupant/11th lord's associate [PD XVI.29;
  HJH2 p372–379]. Gains must be read with the 2nd, "since the 11th necessarily comprises the 2nd" [HJH2 p393], and
  also the lagna, 9th and 10th, from both lagna and Moon [HJH2 p399].
- **Retention** = 2nd house and lord; weak/debilitated 2nd lord aspected by malefics → financial strain [HPA p51].
- **Dhana signatures**: lords of 1, 2, 5, 9, 11 linked in kendras/konas; 2nd–11th exchange; lagna lord in 2nd with 2nd
  lord in 11th [HJH2 p373–374; HPA p60]. Sarvashtakavarga: 11th > 10th, 12th < 11th, lagna > 12th [PD XXIV.38; HPA p103].
- **Outflow and debt**: 12th (expense, fines), 6th (loans, disputes), 8th (sudden loss, shared money); losses come in
  periods of the 11th lord joined with the 12th lord, and of lagna/4th/9th lords in the 8th [HJH2 p393].
- Benefic in the 11th → honest gains; malefic → unscrupulous gains [HJH2 p372]. Say "more questionable sources" only
  with caution; never accuse.
- Higher income can coincide with higher expense; describe both.

## 6. Timing career and money events

Significators for the 10th/11th/2nd per [timing.md](timing.md) §1. Dasha–bhukti lords in 3/11 or kendras from each
other favour rise; 6/8 or 2/12 hinder [HJH2 p260, p364]. A kingdom (authority) comes in periods of the planet in the
lagna, the planet in the 10th, or the strongest planet; it is lost in periods of planets in enemy or debilitation signs
[HJH2 p344; BJ p158]. The 10th-lord dasha by placement and the 11th/12th-lord dasha tables are in HJH2 p261–266,
p379–382, p434–438 (retrieve with `scripts/search.py` when needed). Transit Jupiter over the natal dasha and bhukti
lords often starts the good results within a long bhukti [LOL p364–365].

## 7. Lal Kitab layer

- **10th house**: Saturn's pakka ghar; "foundation of luck"; depends on houses 3, 5 and 9 [KB B3]. **Deceitful 10th**
  [KB B7 p47–48]: bad in 8 means bad in 10; a benefic in 2 makes 10 benefic; good 2 with bad 8 gives good results first,
  then bad; empty 10 → the 4th-house planet gives no effect; Mercury, Rahu or Ketu in 10 depend on Saturn. Debilitated,
  mutually hostile planets in 10 make a "blind chart" [KB B7 p41, p48].
- **2nd house**: Jupiter's seat; 8 empty is good, 2 and 8 both empty excellent; weak 2 rescued by 4 (Moon/mother); if 10
  is empty, 2 sleeps [KB B3 H2].
- **11th house**: any planet here acts like Saturn; "place of justice"; bridge from 9 to 2 [KB B3 H11].
- **6th**: planets in 2 go dormant if 6 is empty or nothing aspects 12 [KB B3 H6].
- **Planet-effect vs house-effect** decides whether a problem is treated as remediable [KB B7 p47].
- Planet-in-house entries (KB B8) give work/wealth themes per placement with conditions; read the conditions, not just
  the headline. Planet years (Jupiter 16, Sun 22, Moon 24, Venus 25, Mars 28, Mercury 34, Saturn 36, Rahu 42, Ketu 48)
  mark ages when a planet's matters mature in Lal Kitab [KB B7 p51] — a supporting timing signal only.
- Annual chart: where the natal 10th/2nd/11th planets and Saturn/Jupiter fall in the age-year's Varshaphal.

## 8. Alternatives and boundaries

Offer distinguishable expressions: internal promotion vs new employer; responsibility without pay rise; self-employment
vs salaried; study/retraining as the "career event"; relocation for work. Salary figures, offer counts, company names,
stock tips and "right time to invest" are outside astrology — say so and point to real-world evidence.
