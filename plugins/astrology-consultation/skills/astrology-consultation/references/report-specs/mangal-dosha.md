# Mangal Dosha report spec (`mangal-dosha`)

Where Mars sits under the four rules the engine computes, what cancels it under each rule, how common each rule is in
random charts, and what it means in plain tendencies. Inside the shared frame ([README.md](README.md)). The dosha is a
convention that the sources do not agree on (CR-V16, CR-N04): this report shows the four rules side by side and never
reduces them to one verdict. It reads Mars's drive and intensity in close ties; it says nothing about how a marriage
will go and never uses fear framing (owner decision 3, 2026-11-20).

**Order (standard):** `rules` → `meaning` → `steadiers`

Inputs: `birth` (date, time, place, `time_reliability`). Model: `report_type: "mangal-dosha"`, `depth: "standard"`, the
frame fields. No timeline rows and no dates in the text: the dosha is read from the birth chart and is not timed.
`ranking` is optional (rows per rule: `item`, `support`, `why`); nothing is graded Strong. Required sentence in `scope`:
"This reads Mars's placement under four named rules as a tendency toward drive and intensity in close ties; it says
nothing about how a marriage will go."

## 1. Sections and computed fields

| Section | Content | Computed fields → engine tool |
|---|---|---|
| `rules` | exactly four units, in this order, one per engine rule: classical (BPHS), popular, Phaladeepika, HJH2. Each unit says whether the rule is met, from which reference points (lagna, Moon, Venus where the rule counts them), its base rate in words, and, in the `counter` line, the cancellations that rule lists (or "no cancellation listed") | `prepare_consultation` (the Mangal convention lines `F.yoga.KUJA-BPHS80`, `KUJA-POPULAR`, `KUJA-PD11_3`, `KUJA-HJH2`: holds, detail, exemptions); Mars's house from the lagna, Moon and Venus → `calculate_birth_chart` |
| `meaning` | what Mars's placement means in plain tendencies (drive, directness, impatience, intensity in close ties), by the house the rules count; where the four rules agree and where they differ | house of Mars → `calculate_birth_chart`; the rule lines above |
| `steadiers` | what steadies Mars's drive in daily life: pace, exercise, naming irritation early, shared routines. No remedies (below) | from the sections above |

`rules` unit themes name the rule: "Classical rule (BPHS)", "Popular rule", "Phaladeepika rule", "HJH2 rule". The
engine's four ids map to them in that order. `spec_checks` refuses a `rules` section that does not hold exactly these
four units, in this order.

Base rates (engine, `chart_facts.BASE_RATE`, regenerated by `tools/calc/yoga_baserates.py`): classical about 15 in 100
random charts, popular about 88 in 100 (about 9 in 10: the plan's figure is confirmed by the engine), Phaladeepika
about 10 in 100, HJH2 about 78 in 100. They count the bare condition before any cancellation, in 4,000 random charts
(random moment 1950-2010, Lahiri, whole-sign houses): a frequency of a configuration, never of an outcome, and random
dates and places are not a population of real births (yoga-definitions.md). Write them in words ("about 88 in 100
random charts"), never as a percentage sign. `spec_checks` requires each rule unit to carry its base rate in words or
the words "base rate not checked". A high rate means the bare condition carries little weight on its own.

Birth-time sensitivity for every unit: `get_birth_time_sensitivity`.

## 2. Method rules and their sources

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| Classical rule: Mars in the 12th, 4th, 7th or 8th from the lagna, unaspected by and unassociated with any benefic; the effect ceases when both people have the yoga (needs both charts) | BPHS p955 (ch. 80 sl. 47-49 and the translator's note) |
| Popular rule: Mars in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th from the lagna, Moon or Venus; "not mentioned in any traditional authoritative text"; Moon with Mars cancels it; own or exalted Mars modifies it (experts disagree) | LOL p329-331 |
| Phaladeepika rule: Mars in the 7th, or the 7th sign and its navamsa both Mars's (needs the ascendant degree; otherwise "undetermined") | PD p142 (XI.3) |
| HJH2 rule: Mars in the 2nd, 12th, 4th, 7th or 8th from the lagna, Moon or Venus (no 1st), counted by bhava; sign exemptions per house (2nd in Gemini or Virgo; 12th in Taurus or Libra; 4th in Aries or Scorpio; 7th in Capricorn or Cancer; 8th in Sagittarius or Pisces), none in Leo or Aquarius, and Mars with Jupiter or the Moon cancels it | HJH2 p70 |
| The sources attach a grave outcome for the partner to these placements. This practice does not read or repeat it (owner rule 2026-11-15). The report says what Mars's drive means as a tendency and what steadies it | owner rule |
| The classical text exists (BPHS), so LOL's "not in any authoritative text" is too strong for the 12/4/7/8 form; the popular 1/2 addition is the part with no classical text | CR-N04, CR-V16 (contradiction-register.md) |

Cite them in `appendix.sources` as "BPHS: Parasara, tr. R. Santhanam, *Brihat Parasara Hora Sastra*, p955", and in unit
`rule_refs` as "BPHS p955". The engine's own source lines say "not page-checked" for the source-map pages; the pages
above were read in the library on 2026-11-20 (the page text of BPHS p955, LOL p329-330, HJH2 p70 and PD p142).

## 3. Conventions

Lahiri, whole-sign houses, mean nodes (the engine defaults). The HJH2 rule counts by bhava; here it is counted by
whole sign, and the unit says so. Mars's own or exalted sign is stated where the popular rule names it as a modifier
(experts disagree: say so; never settle it). The partner and age exceptions the popular rule mentions are not computed
by this report: name them as "not assessed" in the `counter` line.

## 4. Caveats

- No marriage-failure, separation or partner-harm wording, and no illness or death words for anyone (the shared list
  `medical_words.py` refuses them; `spec_checks` adds a Mangal gate: no divorce, separation, "harm to the
  spouse", "the marriage will fail", no "must marry a Manglik" or any match rule).
- No fear framing (danger, ruin, curse, fear, "bad years": the batch 4 gate `_FEAR` is reused).
- Four rules, never one verdict: where they differ, say they differ. Nothing is graded Strong. A rule that is met is
  graded at most Moderate on the form it takes in life, and the common rules (popular, HJH2) are Weak on their own.
- No remedies: no gems, mantras, rituals, fasting, donations or any match rule in this report. The shared remedy rule
  (remedy-safety.md: Parashari only, on request, no gems or costly rituals, a "no efficacy established" line) applies in
  a separate answer if the person asks; the `steadiers` section is daily habits only. `spec_checks` refuses remedy words.
- The "partner with the same dosha" cancellation needs both charts: it belongs to the match report (batch 6) and is named
  here as "not assessed".
- Parashari only: no Lal Kitab ([README.md](README.md) §5).

## 5. What to drop

| Data | Drop |
|---|---|
| approximate time | every unit whose rule counts from the lagna carries `sensitivity`; drop a rule whose count changes inside the birth-time range (the engine marks the Mangal line time-dependent) |
| unknown time | drop `rules` and `meaning` (all four rules need the lagna or houses; the engine reports "not assessed: depends on the birth time" when the count varies); `steadiers` stays in general terms |
| any time | the two-chart cancellation; the partner and age exceptions; any timing |
