# Remedy safety rules

Load only when the user asks for remedies (*upay*), or asks whether a remedy they have heard of is safe. Remedies are
traditional cultural practices; no efficacy is established and none replaces medical, psychological, legal or
financial help. Never pressure, never sell, never make fear the reason.

## 1. Gate: may a remedy be discussed at all?

1. The user asked. (Do not volunteer remedies in an ordinary reading.)
2. The problem is framed astrologically, not as a medical/legal emergency. If a health, fertility or mental-health
   issue is involved, say first that professional care comes first.
3. **Lal Kitab applicability — disputed (CR-L01).** Shrimali/KB: remedies work only where a planet gives the *house
   effect*; pakka-ghar planets cannot be stopped; Sun, Moon or Jupiter in 6 cannot be remedied [KB B7 p42, p47, p67].
   LK1952: planets in own house, exaltation/debilitation or pakka ghar are the *doubtful* (remediable) class and the rest
   are fixed; in house 6 every planet *except* Sun, Jupiter and Moon is fixed [LK1952 p59–60, p91, p176]. When this
   decides the answer, say the sources disagree; never tell someone "nothing can be done" on this basis alone.
4. **Donation check (X17)**: never donate the objects of a planet exalted in the chart; never accept the objects of a
   debilitated one [KB B1, p208] — even though p210 says "donate the planet's objects". Check exaltation by house first.
5. **Prohibited acts for the placement** [KB B13 p208–209] (e.g. Moon in 6: don't donate milk; Venus in 9: the book
   says don't adopt) — present these as the book's advice, never as obligations or threats.

Process rules the book states [KB B1, C2]: 43 days unbroken (restart if broken); daylight only unless the remedy is a
night remedy; the person performs it (or touches the items for a proxy); family (rin) remedies with equal
contributions; debt-planet remedies before that planet's age-year. "One remedy at a time with 1–2 weeks' gap" is from
the debt chapter; applying it generally is a cautious extension (❓ scope).

## 3. Harmless symbolic alternatives [DESIGN]

When a source remedy is prohibited or unsafe, you may offer an alternative **only** if you say it is a modern safe
substitute and not what the book says. Examples: instead of ivory — a small elephant figure in a natural material or
support for elephant-conservation charities; instead of milk to snakes — donating milk to children or a temple; instead
of floating oil — donating oil to someone in need; instead of the 52-day separation — nothing (maternal–infant contact
is never to be restricted); instead of prolonged fasts — a simple act of charity on the planet's weekday. Never invent a
new "Lal Kitab remedy".

## 4. Deterministic content never to present as forecast (KB C5)

"Age 12 days", "death by bullet", "days to death" signs, "only 2 years of age", "mother will die in childhood",
"widowhood … true beyond doubt", "death of wife", "father's death certain if native builds 3 houses",
impotence/infertility claims, caste-linked statements — and the equivalent classical Parashari statements listed in each
source map's §8. If a user asks what the book says, attribute it and add that it is not a prediction.

## 5. Parashari remedial practice

Classical texts prescribe shanti (prayer, gifts, japa, worship) for bad periods and some tithi-based remedies
[PD XXVI.49; PD XII.16–18; PM2 XVIII.38]. Light on Life frames remedies as effective mainly for *non-fixed* karma, and
fixed (dridha) karma as largely unchangeable [LOL p57–65] — a useful honest framing: remedies are for reflection and
steadiness, not guaranteed outcomes. Gemstone prescriptions are not in this library's Parashari sources in a form this
skill can verify; do not prescribe gems or costly rituals. Never say a misfortune is caused by failing to do a remedy.
