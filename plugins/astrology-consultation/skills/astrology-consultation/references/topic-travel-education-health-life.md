# Relocation and foreign travel · education · health symbolism · spirituality · major changes · annual forecast

Procedure from [synthesis-method.md](synthesis-method.md); timing from [timing.md](timing.md).

## 1. Relocation, foreign travel and residence

Separate **trip**, **study/work stint**, **long residence/settlement** and **return home**.

- Houses: 3 short trips; 7 travel/foreign dealings; 9 long journeys; 12 distant residence; 4 home (a residence change
  must touch the 4th) [HJH2 p225].
- Signs: movable and dual signs on the 9th/12th or holding their lords favour travel; watery signs for crossing seas;
  fixed signs favour staying [HJH2 p223–224, p464].
- **Residence abroad**: 12th lord in 9th, 4th or lagna; lagna lord in 9th or 12th; a movable 12th holding the Moon or
  Mercury — with benefic aspect, residence and good fortune; with malefic hemming, a forced departure [HJH2 p418–421].
  Lagna lord strong in a fixed sign/kendra → trips but not residence; lagna lord in the 6th → many journeys, no long stay
  [HJH2 p228, p464–465]. "Unless the ascendant and other planets warrant residence abroad, the dashas by themselves
  cannot give it" [HJH2 p228].
- **Purpose from the planets involved**: 4th/9th lords study; 9th/10th career (+6th: employer-sponsored); 11th lord
  business; Venus/7th lord after marriage; weak lagna lord + 6th medical; 6th/8th forced departure [HJH2 p225–226].
- Earnings abroad: 10th sign or 10th lord's navamsa sign occupied/aspected by planets other than its lord [PD V.9];
  Rahu linked to the 11th or to the Sun → foreign employers/earnings [HJH2 p285, p375].
- Sahams (Paradesha, Jalapatana) are Tajika tools Raman "can be tried" — his two statements of the Paradesha formula
  differ (CR-V10); use only as a labelled supplement.
- **Timing**: 9th-house significator list plus the 12th lord (esp. in a dual sign) [HJH2 p464–465]; slower of dasha/
  bhukti lords shows the overall tenor, the faster the individual trips [LOL p373].
- **Lal Kitab**: Ketu signifies travel; annual-chart Ketu gives travel results by house (1 plans without travel … 12
  progress without travel) [KB B17 p225–226]; Moon in 1 or 12 and Sun in 12 carry "abroad" themes in B8; Rahu/Saturn
  entries mention foreign wealth. Treat Ketu-in-annual-7th "forced journey" language (with its death clause) as C5.
- Visas, jobs and finances are real-world facts; say so.

## 2. Education and learning

- Houses: 4 foundational schooling; 5 intelligence and learning; 9 higher study; 2 speech/early learning; 3 skills.
  Karakas: Mercury (analysis), Jupiter (knowledge). Varga: D24 if supplied and stable.
- 5th a Mercury sign or with Mercury, or 5th lord well placed → sharp intellect [PD XVI.15]; Saraswati yoga (Venus,
  Jupiter, Mercury in kendra/kona/2nd with strong Jupiter) → learning and expression [PD VI.26–27]; benefics in the 4th
  with its lord well placed → higher education, kind by dignity [HPA p50–52].
- Admission, exam results and degree completion are distinct events; no score or admission guarantee.
- Lal Kitab: 5th house = intellect/education; broken education is listed among "bad Jupiter" signs and pitri-rin
  symptoms [KB B8 Jupiter general; B12].

## 3. Health (traditional symbolism only)

**This is not medical advice.** A chart cannot diagnose, predict disease, set severity, time recovery or choose
treatment. Any real symptom or concern goes to a qualified clinician.

What can be said, gently and only if asked: the lagna, lagna lord and Moon describe vitality and resilience as the
tradition sees it; the 6th/8th/12th describe strain, recovery and rest; periods of 6th/8th lords are traditionally seen
as demanding; routine, rest and timely check-ups are sensible in such periods. Phaladeepika itself says a disease should
be predicted only when two, three or more independent yogas point to it [PD XIV.1]; this skill goes further and states
no disease at all. Lal Kitab disease chains and body-part tables [KB B10] and the longevity chapter [KB B11] are
cultural content — never forecasts (KB Part C1, C5).

## 4. Spirituality

5th (practice, mantra), 9th (teachers, dharma), 12th (retreat, liberation), 8th (inquiry); Jupiter and Ketu; D20 if
supplied. Renunciation yogas colour the whole chart when dominant [LOL p418; BJ XV]. No claims about spiritual rank,
past lives or curses.

## 5. Major life changes

Compare outgoing and incoming dasha networks: what they activate (ten channels, [timing.md](timing.md) §2), their
relationship, and which topic houses they share. A dasha change alone does not guarantee upheaval. Dasha–bhukti 6/8 or
2/12, Saturn-heavy transits over the Moon (sade sati) and Lal Kitab annual charts that move many planets into difficult
houses are *pressure* markers; describe what is being restructured, not a catastrophe.

## 6. Annual forecast (a year ahead)

Build it in layers and keep them labelled:

1. **Parashari**: the MD/AD(s) running during the year (split at AD boundaries), what each activates; the year's slow
   transits (Jupiter, Saturn, nodes) against natal Moon and lagna; Tajika annual chart only if a return chart is
   calculated ([timing.md](timing.md) §4).
2. **Lal Kitab Varshaphal**: run `scripts/varshaphal.py` with the natal LK house map for the relevant age-year(s);
   record the running vs completed convention or show both; years 67/99/104 show both source readings. Read which
   planets reach annual H1 (they act first on their natal house), where Ketu falls (travel), and illness markers only as
   the book's symbolism [KB B17].
3. **Converge by theme**: pick the two or three life areas where layers agree; give windows by dasha/bhukti and transit
   months; state disagreements. Month rule in Lal Kitab (house 1 = birth month) is ambiguous (R42) — say so rather than
   convert it to exact dates.
4. Do not fill every month; a year usually has one or two real themes.
