## 1. Split the question first — relationship level

Formation (meeting/commitment), formalisation (marriage), quality (harmony, support), continuity (stability,
separation risk) and partner description are **separate propositions** with separate grades. Never assume gender,
orientation, marriage preference or cultural norms; the classical texts are written for a male native and a wife —
translate to "partner". Fix the **level** before choosing factors [R3-RP1]; do not answer a dating question with
marriage logic or a marriage question with 5th-house logic alone:

| Level | Primary (Parashari) | Supporting | Other schools (own note) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Attraction, infatuation | Venus, Moon, 5th and its lord | Rahu contacts (compulsive pull); Venus/Mars transits over natal Venus/Moon | — |
| Dating, romance | 5th + 11th (desire fulfilled); 5th lord's period | planets in/aspecting the 5th | Jaimini A7 (KP 5-11 needs KP cusps — not available here) |
| Emotional bond | Moon; 4th (heart); 4th from the 7th | Moon–Venus links in D9 | — |
| Serious, committed | 7th, its lord, Venus; D9 7th and its lord | 2nd (family), 11th (fulfilment) joining | Jaimini DK |
| Engagement, marriage | 7th + 2nd + 11th in the same period; D9 support | slow transits on the network | Jaimini UL and 2nd from UL (KP 2-7-11 not available here) |
| Separation, strain | 12th and 6th from the 7th's view (6th, 12th), 8th from 7th (2nd) afflicted | malefics on the network in D9 | (KP 1-6-10-12 not available here) |
| Short-lived bond | 5th/7th active **without** 2nd/11th or D9 support | movable signs, fast transits | — |

Houses: romance and dating lean on the 5th; formal commitment on the 7th and 2nd (family addition) and 11th
(fulfilment); continuity on the 8th (the bond itself — Raman's *mangalya*) and 12th (bed comforts, loss)
[HJH2 p56–57]. Reading romance from the 5th is common modern practice rather than a rule located in this library.

**Meeting and marrying are different events** with different activations, often years apart [R3-RP2; R1-P9]. Give a
*meeting / relationship* window (5th/7th lords, Venus periods; less reliable) separately from a *commitment / marriage*
window (7th + 2nd/11th, D9). Never collapse them into one date.

**Lead with one main window, then the ladder.** Decide which event the question asks about — a relationship
(forming: meeting someone, or a bond becoming serious) or marriage (formalising: engagement, marriage, living as a
committed couple). The first two sentences name *that* window with its grade. Then give the ladder, each rung with its
own window, basis and grade: (1) the promise (natal, no date); (2) forming; (3) formalising. Name rungs by the event —
"when a relationship is likeliest to start", "when commitment is likeliest" — never "first window" and "clearest
window" side by side. Each rung's main window needs a period lord in a primary role for that rung ([timing.md](timing.md) §1) — forming: Venus, the 5th or 7th lord, the
7th lord's dispositor, or a 7th-house occupant; formalising: the 7th lord, its dispositor or Venus together with a 2nd/11th or navamsa-7th link. A sub-period
with no such link, or a transit alone, is supporting nuance, not a main window. Rungs are sequential: the formalising
window peaks after the forming window; if they overlap, say why in one clause. A further supportive period is a *fallback* with its reason ("if 2027–28 passes, the next
opening is 2029–30 because…"), not a second main window. Describe a partner as tendencies (§6).

## 2. Parashari network

**For an existing relationship** (dating, living together, "will this last?") also read the **5th house and its
lord** alongside the 7th network. Reading romance and affection from the 5th is common modern practice rather than a
rule located in this library (label it as outside knowledge if it carries weight); the library's 5th-house rules concern
intelligence, pleasure and children. Note the classical *caution* on 5th–7th links: 7th lord in the 5th or 5th lord in
the 7th "may not marry, or if married childless" [HJH2 p13; PD X.2] — deterministic, never a
forecast, but it means a 5th–7th link is not automatically a supportive sign. Continuity rests on the 7th, 8th and Venus
below.

**Primary factors** [HJH2 p8–9; PM2 XX.3]: the 7th house, the 7th lord, Venus (kalatra karaka) — then occupants and
aspects of the 7th and planets joining the 7th lord. Repeat the 7th and 7th lord **from the Moon** [HJH2 p20]. Jupiter
is a key protector (and, in the classical female-chart convention, the husband's significator); use it as a general
stabiliser, not a gendered rule [PD XI.1; HJH2 p46].

**Supportive signatures** (grade by how many repeat):
- 7th from lagna or Moon occupied/aspected by the 9th lord, its own lord or benefics [HJH2 p12; PD X.1].
- 2nd, 7th and 12th lords in kendras/konas aspected by Jupiter; benefics 2nd/7th/11th from the 7th lord [PD X.9–10].
- 7th lord or Venus in benefic signs/navamsas, aspected by Jupiter; strong 7th lord in a kendra [PM2 XX.24–30].
- Mutual aspect of 7th lord and Venus holds a marriage together even with other strain [HJH2 p39].
- Raman rejects the "karaka in its own house destroys it" rule for Venus in the 7th: in his experience one of the
  finest placements for a fairly happy marriage [HJH2 p65] (CR-V02 records the classical opposite).

**Strain signatures**: 7th, 7th lord and Venus all afflicted (malefic-joined/aspected, hemmed, combust, debilitated, in
6/8/12 or in enemy signs) — the PD XV.6 "confluence" case [LOL p288–289; PM2 XX.6]; malefics 4th/8th/12th from Venus
[PD X.1]; Mars–Venus without Jupiter's steadying influence [HJH2 p64]; 6/8/12 lords on the 7th/lord/karaka rule out
*early* marriage [HJH2 p26] (a *different* planet must make the contact — Venus owning the 6th does not "touch"
itself; count that only as a weakness of the karaka). Afflictions to the 8th and 8th lord by nodes/Mars/Saturn in rasi and navamsa mark
unconventional unions (love, inter-community, divorcee partner) [HJH2 p76] — describe as "outside convention", never
as a moral judgement, and work out what it means in this chart with the test below.

**"Unconventional": find what it refers to in this chart.** Raman uses the word for a marriage outside what custom
approved in his setting: by the couple's own choice or across the family's social group [HJH2 p71]; his cases extend
it to a partner from another faith or country, or one married before [HJH2 p72–76]. His test is the 8th house **and** its lord afflicted by Rahu, Ketu, Mars or Saturn, in rasi **and**
navamsa [HJH2 p76]; his cases add Ketu in the 7th, Rahu on Venus, and malefics in or hemming the 8th from the Moon
[HJH2 p72–77]. Never repeat the label bare. Name the factor that raised it, then score each possible meaning by its
own indicators in this chart:

| Possible meaning | Its own indicators |
|---|---|
| Form of the union (own choice, outside the family's circle, partner married before) | Raman's 8th-house test and his supporting factors, above |
| Partner's background (another culture or country) | Saturn or Rahu tied to the 7th or its lord [LOL p401]; malefics in watery signs on the 8th with Jupiter's influence, one case [HJH2 p74–75]; 7th–12th links |
| Courtship (how it starts) | Rahu in or on the 5th [HJH1 p221–222]; 5th–7th activity without the 2nd/11th (§1) |
| Living arrangement (distance, not formalised, late) | 7th active without 2nd/11th or D9 support (§1); Saturn's delay (below) |
| Intimate life | 12th house, its lord, Venus, Rahu in the 12th (§9) |
| Not about relationships | the 10th lord tied to the 12th and afflicted: unusual work [HJH2 p266] → topic-career-money |

Count distinct indicators (one placement counts once). Give the best two meanings, each with its indicators, and one
clause on why the others are weaker. Counter-factors: Jupiter's aspect on the 7th, 8th or Venus, or a strong 7th lord
with benefics, keeps the form conventional. Saturn and Mars aspect several signs each, so a single touch on the 8th is
common (arithmetic, not a book rule): house and lord, in both charts, = supported; one of them = possible, graded
Weak. Raman calls such unions "usually" unaccepted for his time and place, and much of what he lists is ordinary
today: say so, and explain the possible meanings yourself. Do not ask about, name or predict caste, community or
religion unless the person raises it. The chart is not read for sexual orientation or gender identity; the claim of
that kind on HJH1 p222 is not used.

**Delay versus denial.** Saturn's aspect on 7th, 7th lord and Venus delays marriage *unless the dasha lord is very
strong*; a strong 7th lord and strong Venus "cannot push it off too far" [HJH2 p26, p32]; Saturn owning the 7th does not
delay [HJH2 p33]. Classical "denial" requires the whole triad (plus lagna lord) to fail together [PM2 XX.6]. Without
that, say delay, not denial.

**Divisional check.** D9 for quality and continuity: the 7th lord in 6/8/12 from navamsa lagna intensifies difficulty;
dual-sign 7th lord and Venus in rasi or navamsa point to more than one significant partnership [HJH2 p24, p29; PD X.5].
Raman: consult the navamsa only after the rasi is satisfactory — if D1 itself shows no happiness, D9 cannot supply it
[PM2 p26]. A stable birth time is needed for the navamsa lagna.

**Number of marriages / remarriage** rules [HJH2 p15–16; PM2 XX.37–46] are classical and gendered. Modern reading
(Raman): remarriage after divorce or death, or a significant second relationship [PM2 p27, p64–65]. Only mention when
asked and when several signatures repeat.

**Kuja dosha**: disputed; three conventions, never merged — BPHS 80.47–49 (12/4/7/8 from the lagna, benefic aspect cancels), popular LOL form (1/2/4/7/8/12 from lagna, Moon, Venus; "non-authoritative"), PD XI.3 (Mars in the 7th); see CR-N04, CR-V16 and yoga-definitions.md. Very common, many cancellations (both partners have it; Mars own/
exalted; Moon–Mars; after ~28–30; certain signs) [LOL p329–331; HJH2 p70]. Never present it as a marriage verdict.
Raman's matching table penalises the woman's chart more — do not reproduce that asymmetry as advice.

**Compatibility** (two charts supplied): basic chart structure matters more than kuta points [HJH2 p66; PM2 p107].
Harmonious Venus/Mars/Jupiter links (trine or 3/11), Sun–Moon harmony, one partner's Moon sign as the other's lagna
[HJH2 p69–70]. Kutas rest only on Moon signs/stars and are a separate traditional module: `get_compatibility` gives
the Prasna Marga XXI sign and star counts, each with the verse that reads it and the authorities that disagree. It
gives no unit scores from PM2 (p107). Its `kootas` block adds four Ashtakoota kootas whose rules the sources agree on
— tara, bhakoot, graha maitri, nadi — each with the points the Muhurta Chintamani table assigns, its maximum, the
exceptions as conditions, and the readings that disagree (not page-checked by the owner). Vashya,
yoni, gana and varna are not computed (sources conflict), so there is no 36-point total: never add the four up, never
present a partial sum as a score. Get both people's consent before calculating, and never turn a kuta reading into a
marriage verdict.

## 3. Timing marriage

Significators [HJH2 p20–31; PM2 XX.65]: 7th lord; planets in/aspecting the 7th; planets aspecting/joined to the 7th
lord; dispositors of the 7th lord in rasi and navamsa; Venus; the Moon; the 2nd lord's dispositor; 7th lord from the
Moon; 9th/10th lords "if earlier periods are fruitless"; navamsa-lagna lord; Rahu (Raman: logic unclear but widely
accepted). Grade dasha–bhukti pairs with the intensity table; drop ages that do not fit; prefer the strongest remaining
lord. Transit: Jupiter over the 7th lord's rasi/navamsa sign or trines; Venus, lagna lord, Moon-sign lord or 7th lord
over the 7th [PM2 XX.66]; Jupiter on the sign of (lagna + 7th) longitudes [HJH2 p26, p462]. Early vs late tendency:
7th lord or Venus with the lagna lord or in kendras → early; 7th lord far from 1st and 7th → later [PM2 XX.59–62];
"early/late" is relative to the person's society and era [HJH2 p32].

## 4. Lal Kitab layer (separate evidence note)

Use natal Lal Kitab houses (whole-sign from lagna, fixed-Aries labels). KB section numbers refer to the owner's private
knowledge base (not distributed in the public edition): provenance notes, not sources you can open.

- **House 7 logic** [KB B3 H7, p67]: the 7th aspects nothing; only house 1 aspects it, so an empty 1st leaves the 7th
  "sleeping". The 7th follows Venus's position; Venus acts like Mercury here; Venus + Mercury together make Saturn
  "superior" (wealth). Moon in 7 with more than two planets shifts Moon's harm to Jupiter.
- **Karakas**: Venus (+ Mercury) for the 7th; Venus and Mercury share pakka ghar 7 [KB R03, B4].
- **Scapegoat chain**: afflicted Saturn and afflicted Mercury pass their trouble to Venus (wife/partner) [KB B7 p48–50].
  Read this as "strain shows up in the relationship", not as a partner's fate.
- **Sleeping planet**: a planet with nothing in the 7th from it "acts only after marriage" (e.g. Jupiter in 1 with 7
  empty) [KB B7 p44] — a Lal Kitab timing hint.
- **Planet-in-house cautions with ages** (as the book states them; present as tradition, not fact): Moon in 1 —
  marriage before 28 said to hurt parents; Moon in 7 — no marriage before 24; Sun bad in 1 with 7 empty — early
  marriage as remedy [KB B8 p74, p81, p84]. Venus's planet-year is 25 [KB B7 p51].
- **Stree rin (wife's debt)** signature: Sun, Rahu or Ketu in 2 (text) vs Sun/Moon/Rahu in 2/7 (summary table) — cite
  both (X21) [KB B12 p170, p173].
- **Mangalik** (Shrimali's list: Mars in 1/4/7/8/12 — the popular form, not BPHS 80.47–49) appears in Shrimali as a [VED] rule reproduced by the author, not a Lal Kitab
  doctrine [KB B9]. The author himself criticises mechanical Mangal-dosh customs.
- **Annual chart**: where natal 7th-house planets and Venus land in the Varshaphal for the relevant age-years
  (`scripts/varshaphal.py`); a planet reaching annual H1 acts first on its natal house [KB B17].
- Many B8 entries contain "death of wife", "widowhood" or infertility statements — never forecasts (KB Part C5).

## 5. Form questions: how they meet, who initiates, new vs past person, second marriage

- **How will they meet?** Rank two or three settings by how many *independent* indicators agree — the 7th lord's
  house and associations, its dispositor, the D9 7th and its lord, the house the running period lords activate, the
  transit contacts in the window (Jaimini DK/UL in their own note). Settings: 1st direct encounter; 3rd messaging,
  neighbourhood, short trips, siblings; 4th family circle, home; 5th study, creative, leisure; 6th colleagues,
  service; 9th higher study, teachers, travel, religion; 10th work; 11th friends, networks, groups, introductions;
  12th abroad, distance, online/private. Say "more likely through X or Y than Z"; never narrate a scene
  [R3-RP3]. A route supported by one placement only is "possible".
- **Who approaches first?** No credible method in the library or in practice [R3-RP6]. Say so briefly, then answer
  the nearest useful question: does the current period favour *the person* taking initiative (strong lagna lord,
  3rd/5th/7th-linked Mars or Venus periods, lagna lord activated by transit)?
- **Will a past person return / new vs past?** Ask (or infer) the relationship type, how long ago it ended and
  whether contact continues. Answer as a *range*: contact without reunion, reconciliation, brief reunion and another
  break; do not rank these as more or less common (no source in the library ranks them). Continuity is favoured when the running period
  re-activates the same relationship network that was active in the earlier episode (same period lords, the 12th/8th
  or Ketu involved); a new person when the periods activate the 5th/7th/11th freshly or Rahu [R3-RP4, RP5]. Without the
  other person's chart, keep it at Moderate at most.
- **Second marriage**: the schools disagree (9th; 2nd + 9th; 8th from UL/A7; 11th). Use one rule, name it, never
  stack several "second-marriage houses" [R3-RP7].
- **Friendship turning romantic**: an 11th–5th or 11th–7th link is supportive texture only, low confidence [R3-RP6].

## 6. Partner description and appearance (tendencies)

The classical texts describe the spouse's **temperament, age and fortune** far more than looks: from the planet owning
the 7th sign or its navamsa — Saturn older/serious, Mars spirited/irascible, Venus attractive and fortunate, Mercury
learned and skilled, Moon tender, Jupiter principled and self-controlled, Sun gentle and hard-working [BJ p243,
stated for a woman's chart; apply symmetrically as "partner"]. PM2: the stronger of Venus, the 7th lord and any
7th-house occupant gives the partner's nature; physical features from the stronger of the 7th lord's dispositor and
its navamsa lord; planets joined to Venus colour the partner [PM2 XX.17–21, p89]. Planet body-types come from
BJ II [BJ p52–53] and PD II [PD p42–44]. When the candidates for "the stronger" are close (no clear margin in the
engine's strength output), do not pick one: name both, describe only the traits they share, and grade the description
Weak (the skill's rule, not a book rule).

Method [R3-AP4]:
1. Choose one school's indicators (Parashari: 7th occupants, 7th lord, its dispositor and navamsa lord, D9 7th and
   lord; Jaimini DK in its own note).
2. Report a trait only when **two or more** indicators agree.
3. Lead with temperament, age gap, background or role (better supported). Then give looks from the planet chosen in
   step 1, through its body-type in the texts: build, stature (tall, middling or short) and bearing, two or three
   features at most, as **tendencies**, each with its reason from the chart ("Saturn as that planet: lean and tall");
   name one or two texts in total, in passing, not one per feature.
   Where the texts differ on a feature, say so or leave it out. Complexion only if asked: at most once, neutrally, as the text's
   tendency within the partner's own family and region [BJ p88; SAR1 p158]; never rank or value it, never treat one
   tone as better, no comparisons. No exact measurements. Caste, community, religion or ethnicity are not named, predicted or asked about unless the person raises them; the chart is not read for sexual orientation or gender identity.
4. Add the birth-time caveat when the D9 lagna or 7th cusp is near a boundary.
5. Say once that this is the tradition's description, untested, graded Weak (Moderate only for a trait two indicators
   share). It must never be used to identify, pick or reject a real person.

## 7. Alternative manifestations to offer

Formalising an existing bond vs a new meeting; engagement or cohabitation vs legal marriage; a business partnership or
contract rather than romance (the 7th is also contracts and the public); renegotiation of a relationship rather than
its end; distance (work, relocation) rather than estrangement; a partner from a different background (Saturn/Rahu on
the 7th [LOL p401]).

## 8. Boundaries

Do not state an unknown partner's caste, exact age, profession or looks as fact (tendencies, §6); do not predict
infidelity, divorce or a partner's death; do not tell someone to marry or leave. Another person's feelings and choices
are not in the chart. Intimacy has its own limits (§9). See [communication.md](communication.md).
None of this is lifted for any question; a person's own tendencies are answered in §10.

## 9. Intimacy and sexual life

For an adult asking about their own intimate life: desire, closeness, satisfaction, how it changes. Answer it, in
plain respectful words. The texts call it *sayana sukha*, "bed comforts", and treat it as one of life's comforts, read
from the 12th house [HJH2 p418; JP2 XV.73; PVR p80–81] with Venus as its karaka [PVR p96] and the 7th for union with
the partner [JP2 XIV.1; PVR p80–81]. A natal description first; timing only if asked.

Read in this order, and say what each step shows in this chart:
1. **P — 12th house and its lord**: occupants, aspects, the lord's sign, house and dignity. Supported: the 12th and
   Venus both well disposed; the 12th lord with or aspected by benefics, in benefic vargas, own or exalted [HJH2 p422;
   JP2 XV.79–81]; BPHS states the fullest form only for the Moon as 12th lord (exalted, own, or in the 5th, 9th or
   11th) [BPHS ch. 23 sl. 1–4, PDF p198]. Strained: an afflicted 12th lord, or
   one in the 6th or 8th or in a debilitation or enemy navamsa [HJH2 p422–423; BPHS ch. 23 sl. 5–6].
2. **P — Venus**: dignity, combustion, companions and D9 sign (vedic-natal §4). Venus in the 12th: enjoyment that
   costs something [LOL p175–176]; Mercury with Venus in the 12th supports [JP2 XV.79]; Venus afflicted by the Sun:
   less physical pull in the marriage [HJH2 p19]; the Venus–Mars relation carries physical attraction and needs
   Jupiter's steadying [HJH2 p64].
3. **S — Lagna lord**: in the 6th, 8th or 12th, debilitated, or with Saturn or Rahu, the comfort is held back on the
   person's own side [HJH2 p422–423].
4. **S — 7th lord**: joined or aspected by Venus, or in a Venus sign: strong physical fondness inside the
   partnership [JP2 XIV.42]. The quality of the bond itself is §2.
5. **S — Rahu in the 12th**: wanting that is hard to satisfy, steadied by Jupiter's aspect [LOL p379] — one modern
   author, one case: Weak alone.
6. **S — D9** (stable time): the 12th lord's navamsa confirms or qualifies step 1 [BPHS ch. 23 sl. 5–6]; Venus's
   navamsa is read as in §2.

**Say why, not only whether.** When the comfort is limited, Raman lists the possible causes: circumstance, means,
health, the body, or restraint chosen for spiritual reasons [HJH2 p422–423]. Never choose a health cause for the
person. Rank the others for this chart and give the best two with their support: living apart, travel or work away
(12th factors in movable signs or tied to Saturn [JP2 XV.73]; foreign-residence links); too little rest or privacy
(the 12th is also sleep; a strained 4th); restraint by choice (the skill's own inference, no library rule: Ketu,
Jupiter or Saturn on the 12th in a chart that leans to renunciation); indulgence and its cost (Venus or Rahu in the 12th); the relationship itself
(7th and Venus strained, §2). Mixed results are normal: "desire strong, opportunity limited" is a valid reading.

**Phases, not a verdict.** In Raman's worked case the comfort was normal for about fifteen years and then absent,
because helpful and harmful influences were balanced [HJH2 p474]. If timing is asked, the periods of the 12th lord,
Venus and 12th-house planets bring the theme forward; grade them with [timing.md](timing.md) §1.

**Counter-factors**: Jupiter's aspect on the 12th, its lord or Venus; a strong lagna lord; Raman's caution that
12th-house loss comes fully only when the whole chart agrees [HJH2 p423].
**Grade**: Moderate when the 12th with its lord and Venus agree in rasi and navamsa; Weak on one factor or when they
disagree. No certainty.

**Limits.** Adults only: if the person appears to be a minor, do not discuss sexual topics. Nothing explicit. Nothing
on sexual health, potency or fertility; no diagnosis. The classical texts attach moral labels to these combinations;
do not repeat them and do not turn them into verdicts on character or fidelity. The chart is not read for sexual
orientation or gender identity. Nothing about a named third person's desires or conduct.

## 10. "Does my own chart show relationships outside marriage?"

Owner decision 2026-11-13: answer it; do not refuse. It is a question about the person's **own** tendencies, so read
desire, restlessness, privacy and the periods when temptation or strain runs higher. §8 is unchanged: nothing about a
real partner's conduct, never "you will have an affair", no certainty.

1. **7th house, its lord and Venus.** The 7th covers the spouse and also desire in general and ties beyond the
   marriage [LOL p167], so start there: occupants, aspects, the lord's dignity, in rasi and navamsa (§2).
2. **Named pressures, each said with its book.** Raman: Saturn or Mars aspecting Venus in the 7th, or the 7th lord or
   Venus with a node under a malefic aspect [HJH2 p14]; Venus with Mars and Mercury when afflicted, and Mars with Venus
   as pleasure-seeking [HJH2 p19]. Jataka Parijata: the 2nd, 7th and 10th lords together in the 10th [JP2 XIV.4].
   Present, partly present or absent: say which. Absent is a real answer.
3. **12th house and its lord** for the private side of intimate life, read as in §9.
4. **Counter-factors.** The same Mars-Venus pair reads as a loving and faithful bond when well placed [HJH2 p19];
   Jupiter's aspect and a strong lagna lord steady it (§9). State them with equal weight.
5. **Periods, only if asked**: when the dashas of Venus, the 7th lord or the 12th lord run, the theme is louder;
   windows by [timing.md](timing.md).

Say it as a tendency: "the chart shows a strong pull toward …, steadied by …". The texts use moral labels for these
combinations: do not repeat them, and prefer plain words ("outside the marriage", "pull", "restlessness") to the
labels. Grade Weak on one factor, Moderate only when 7th, Venus and navamsa agree; Unknown where the birth time
leaves the houses open. A tendency is not conduct: the choice stays with the person. Adults only (§9 Limits).
