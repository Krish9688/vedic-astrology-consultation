# Source ledger and disagreements

Research access date: **2026-09-06**. This file records passages actually retrieved, not a claim to have read every book or validated predictive accuracy. Textual authority establishes what a tradition teaches; it does not establish that its forecasts are empirically reliable. References summarize selected principles; no copyrighted book is bundled.

## Tags and source handling

- **CLASSICAL:** a principle located in a named classical work; distinguish the root text from translator/commentator notes.
- **TRADITIONAL:** common teaching used as a working baseline, without implying unanimity.
- **SCHOOL:** a named lineage/teacher's method or interpretation.
- **MODERN:** a contemporary technique/application, often a synthesis of older concepts.
- **EXPERIMENTAL:** disputed or inadequately validated practice; no default predictive authority.
- **DESIGN:** this skill's explicit operational policy, including confidence, missing-data behavior, evidence dependency and sensitive-topic safeguards.

Prefer a located classical text/translation, then established scholarship, identifiable teaching, careful modern investigation, practitioner experience and finally community discussion. Reposts are not independent corroborating authors. Record access failures. Never upgrade a search snippet, sales description or AI-generated translation to a fully examined primary text.

### BJ — Brihat Jataka

Varahamihira; N. Chidambaram Aiyar edition, 1905, identified through [Wikimedia catalog metadata](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Brihat_jataka_(IA_brihatjataka00varaiala).pdf). [Internet Archive OCR](https://archive.org/stream/brihatjataka00varaiala/brihatjataka00varaiala_djvu.txt) was inspected for VIII.1–3, VIII.23 and the following translator's Udu-dasha note (printed pp. 94–97), and IX.1–8 on Ashtakavarga. **CLASSICAL + separately identified commentary.** Old OCR has errors.

VIII.23 addresses conflicting effects; the following note distinguishes a commonly used nakshatra-period scheme from the chapter's earlier system. This supports keeping period systems separate. The note's sequence and star groups were cross-checked against the standard Vimshottari description.

[VIII.20, Sanskrit and Michael D. Neely's 2007 translation](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/brihat-jataka-by-varahamihira-sanskrit-english/d/doc1501706.html) was read separately: it explicitly connects multiple natal significations to dasha interpretation. This is a second translation witness to the synthesis principle, not an independent astrological experiment.

[X.2 hosted by VedicPupil](https://vedicpupil.in/library/brihat-jataka-book-by-varahamihira/karmajiva-ch10/2) was inspected as a supplementary profession passage; its translation provenance is less clear and its Hindi is labeled AI. No AI-generated explanation from that site is treated as authority. P5 independently supplies the relevant livelihood/Navamsa approach.

### PVR — P.V.R. Narasimha Rao

[Vedic Astrology: An Integrated Approach](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf), first published 2000, with 2010 update. **SCHOOL/MODERN synthesis.** Inspected preface, chapter 6/table 11, argala discussion, birth-time-error passages, ethical chapter opening and a prediction case's retrospective-subperiod disclosure. The author explicitly notes later revisions. Supports topical Vargas and attention to time sensitivity; does not certify this skill's confidence labels. Its integration of traditions is itself a methodological position. Old and revised calculation choices must not be merged silently. No personal examples or software from it are imported.

### RATH — Sanjay Rath

[Argala: Planetary Intervention](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/amateur/argala-planetary-intervention/comment-page-1/). **SCHOOL, drawing on traditional sutras.** Primary influence/obstruction pairs, secondary/special extensions, Ketu reversal and alternative expressions were inspected. The package makes only the primary pair structure a readily available optional module; specialist extensions need explicit verification. Fear-based examples and gendered moral judgments are not adopted.

### KNR — K.N. Rao teaching approach

[Publisher's description of Ups and Downs in Career](https://www.journalofastrology.com/product_details.php?item_id=156). **MODERN/SCHOOL; publisher metadata only.** Confirms that the described approach combines Jupiter–Saturn double transit, Vimshottari and case-based synthesis. ### SWE — Swiss Ephemeris

[Official documentation](https://www.astro.com/swisseph/swisseph.htm), especially 2.8 and 6.2. **Technical primary documentation.** Records alternative ayanamsas and house systems. Used only to clarify reproducible input conventions, not to justify astrological outcomes. No library dependency or code was adopted.

### DESIGN sources, not astrology authorities

- [Mermaid](https://mermaid.js.org/intro/), [Typst](https://github.com/typst/typst) and [Letta Code](https://github.com/letta-ai/letta-code) were evaluated and not integrated. Diagrams may use an existing host renderer; neither a compiler nor stateful-agent platform is required to interpret a chart.

The cream/green palette, figure contracts, report modes, revision invalidation and confidence display rules are original DESIGN decisions. They are not classical doctrine or demonstrated predictors. Their purpose is consistency, legibility and honest scope.

## Extending the knowledge responsibly

When asked for a specialist rule absent here, locate the source, record edition/section, distinguish author text from commentary, compare a second substantive account where disputed, and state whether the technique changes the baseline. If inaccessible, be explicit. Never cite an invented verse or build certainty on a random article. Keep new knowledge original and concise; do not download or bundle books merely to make the package appear comprehensive.
